# Track 03 — Full Draft 01: locked-motif form

**Status:** Active full-cue development  
**Tempo / meter:** 76 BPM, 3/4  
**Length:** 64 bars, about 151.6 seconds before tail  
**Canonical motif:** `D-D-F-Bb | A-E-F`  
**Primary motif owner:** clarinet

## Purpose

This is the first complete-form cue built after Theme 07 was explicitly locked as the general theme / motif of Track 03.

Development Lab 01 established that the tested transformation classes all sounded viable. Full Draft 01 therefore chooses among them by dramatic function rather than presenting them as an atlas.

The cue remains intended for lonely / ponderous / thoughtful exploration:

> sitting somewhere high and quiet, thinking about how far you have come, without regretting it.

## Form

### I — bars 1–8: thesis / canonical teaching

- two clear clarinet motif statements;
- sparse cello answers;
- rare high afterimage;
- no large orchestral support.

Function: teach the object before developing it.

### II — bars 9–16: inward adjacent version

- same motif head and rhythmic identity;
- tail turns inward;
- oboe appears only after foreground space opens;
- section grows more introspective rather than denser.

Function: first adjacent emotional state.

### III — bars 17–24: split dialogue / first widening

- clarinet states the head;
- cello completes the tail;
- viola and V2 widen the field without competing for foreground;
- canonical motif is re-established before leaving the section.

Function: thought becomes feeling.

### IV — bars 25–32: development

- repeated-note head is fragmented;
- full-sentence sequencing is avoided;
- canonical identity is then augmented in duration;
- cello supplies long counterweight;
- bar 32 creates an explicit breath.

Function: active consideration rather than another refrain statement.

### V — bars 33–40: emotional widening / motif inheritance

- cello inherits the motif once, one octave lower;
- clarinet becomes sustained horizon color;
- upper strings widen through simple held voice-leading;
- horns appear only as one distant horizon gesture;
- cello reaches the cue's principal expressive apex;
- clarinet then re-establishes the motif.

Function: the thought becomes personally meaningful without turning into Track 00-style revelation.

### VI — bars 41–48: stripped recollection

- near-naked clarinet;
- motif stretched and partially remembered;
- almost no cello;
- extremely sparse piano;
- tiny afterimage / bassoon punctuation only.

Function: deepest loneliness / memory.

### VII — bars 49–56: reassembly

- canonical clarinet refrain returns;
- world-response voices gradually return;
- split dialogue returns as learned vocabulary rather than novelty.

Function: recovery and recognition.

### VIII — bars 57–64: final return / coda

- canonical clarinet refrain;
- sparse cello answer;
- final partial motif statement;
- detail falls away into low B-flat and the final afterimage.

Function: acceptance rather than triumph.

## Orchestration semantics

- **Clarinet:** motif owner / remembered thought.
- **Cello:** emotional answer / expressive apex.
- **Piano:** continuity and harmonic thought motion.
- **Harp & celeste:** rare memory afterimage.
- **Oboe:** occasional environmental response after foreground clears.
- **Bassoon:** low punctuation / distant world presence.
- **Viola / V2 / V1:** widening and reassembly; not continuous pads.
- **Horn:** one distant horizon gesture only.

## Acceptance boundary

This is not a composition freeze.

The next authoritative step is audition of the complete form, preferably followed by a real BBCSO render once the synthetic/form pass stops exposing structural defects.

The main questions are:

1. does the motif remain obvious throughout the complete cue;
2. do transformations serve their dramatic functions rather than sounding like exercises;
3. does the widening around bars 33–40 feel earned but restrained;
4. does the stripped recollection earn the return;
5. does the cue remain thoughtful rather than becoming sleepy or tragic;
6. does the final return feel like recognition rather than reset.
