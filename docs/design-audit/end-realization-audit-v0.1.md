# End Realization Audit v0.1

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-05
**Status:** Detailed gameplay/value audit. Full Skyforge realization is promising but depends on proving enduring expedition/industrial value beyond first Elytra/Shulker/Levitite unlocks.

## Audit question

> Does the End provide enough unique, repeatable, infrastructure-generating gameplay to justify full Skyforge authorship rather than remaining a late-game loot dimension?

**Verdict: PROMISING / CONDITIONAL YES.**

The End has the strongest opportunity to become a genuinely different late-game engineering domain.

It already contains:

- the Dragon ritual progression;
- gateways;
- extreme negative-space travel;
- End Cities/ships;
- Shulkers/Shulker Boxes;
- Elytra;
- chorus;
- End Stone;
- Aeronautics' End Stone -> Levitite technology loop;
- strong expedition/navigation risk.

Its weakness is not uniqueness.

Its unresolved issues are **post-unlock persistence** and **long-range exploration variation**.

Sparse negative space is valuable only if distant expeditions are punctuated by enough distinct behaviors, structures, resources, and phenomena to sustain anticipation.

A player can currently obtain:

~~~text
Dragon victory
-> End gateway
-> End City
-> Shulkers
-> Elytra
~~~

and then have little systemic reason to maintain an End network.

Full Skyforge realization is justified if the End becomes a world the player repeatedly **surveys, stages from, extracts from, engineers for, and expands through**.

## Exploration-worth requirement

The End should remain sparse.

It should **not** remain repetitive.

Desired rhythm:

~~~text
long quiet crossing
    -> distant silhouette / signal / anomaly
    -> expedition commitment
    -> behaviorally distinct site or phenomenon
    -> staging / recovery decision
    -> next horizon
~~~

Variation should come from:

- landmass/negative-space composition;
- unusual mob behavior;
- rare structures;
- resource/process differences;
- environmental phenomena;
- exceptional encounters.

See [Dimension Exploration Enrichment Audit v0.1](dimension-exploration-enrichment-audit-v0.1.md).

## Gameplay identity

Working role:

> **A late-game aeronautical frontier where the player operates beyond the comfortable engineering envelope of the Overworld.**

The End should ask:

~~~text
How far can I safely go?
Where can I land?
How do I navigate home?
What happens if propulsion fails?
What vehicle architecture is appropriate here?
How do I establish a foothold in almost-empty space?
~~~

This is distinct from both:

- Overworld open-sky civilization;
- Nether constrained corridor operations.

## Entry and progression role

The End remains a major ritual/progression boundary.

### Stronghold / End Portal

Overworld progression finds the stronghold and activates the End Portal.

### Central End

Initial arrival should preserve:

- safe spawn platform;
- Dragon arena;
- obsidian pillars/crystals;
- exit portal;
- respawn compatibility;
- gateway generation.

### Dragon victory

Defeating the Dragon:

- activates return;
- spawns End gateway access;
- opens the ordinary outer-End loop.

Additional Dragon kills can create more gateways up to the vanilla limit.

This makes the central End a repeatable ritual hub rather than necessarily a one-time boss room.

## Existing unique capability payoffs

### Elytra — VERY STRONG personal-mobility unlock

Elytra are among vanilla Minecraft's most transformative items.

They create:

- personal aerial travel;
- recovery/scouting;
- rapid movement.

In Skyforge they must coexist with powered aircraft.

Current policy:

- keep Elytra;
- suppress vanilla-style safe sustained firework propulsion;
- retain them as high-performance personal soaring equipment.

This preserves their identity without allowing them to become a cheap universal powered-aircraft substitute.

### Shulker Boxes — VERY STRONG logistics unlock

Shulker Boxes transform personal inventory logistics.

They are a major late-game reward and interact directly with Skyforge freight balance.

Java shulkers are renewable through shulker duplication behavior, so Shulker Shells can support a true repeatable farm rather than only finite city raids.

This gives the End one strong permanent-infrastructure reason:

~~~text
establish Shulker farm
    -> renewable shells
    -> portable storage
~~~

However, once sufficient boxes exist, marginal demand may fall.

Shulkers contribute strong utility but should not be the End's only ongoing economy.

### End Stone -> Levitite — VERY STRONG engineering specialization

Create Aeronautics provides the most important current End-specific engineering hook.

Current source-backed chain:

~~~text
End Stone
    -> crushing
    -> End Stone Powder

4 x End Stone Powder
+ 2 x Zinc Nuggets
+ 500 mB Water
+ HEATED MIXING
    -> 500 mB Levitite Blend

Levitite Blend
+ crystallization catalyst/heat context
    -> Levitite
~~~

Aeronautics' own Ponder behavior demonstrates:

- enough Levitite can keep a simulated contraption afloat;
- Levitite alone cannot make it gain altitude;
- another force source remains necessary;
- Levitite strongly resists low-speed motion, with resistance decreasing at higher speed.

This is an excellent capability reward.

It changes vehicle architecture rather than simply improving a number.

### End Stone abundance is not a problem by itself

End Stone is broadly available.

That does not mean Levitite lacks economic meaning.

The useful cost can come from:

- bulk extraction;
- crushing;
- zinc imports;
- water;
- heated mixing;
- crystallization;
- vehicle-scale material demand.

If large craft require substantial Levitite mass, End Stone quarrying becomes an industrial activity.

If only a handful of Levitite blocks are ever needed, the End Stone loop becomes a one-time recipe fetch.

This must be measured.

### End Cities / ships — STRONG repeated expedition content

End Cities provide:

- Shulkers;
- high-tier loot;
- End Ships;
- Elytra;
- distinctive building material;
- navigation landmarks.

In an effectively unbounded world, new cities support repeated expeditions.

This is **repeatable exploration**, though not renewable local production.

That distinction matters:

~~~text
repeatable expedition
    !=
permanent local economy
~~~

Both are valuable.

A fully realized End can lean more heavily on expeditionary value than the Nether, provided forward bases and aircraft staging make those expeditions meaningfully better.

### Dragon respawning / Dragon's Breath — MODERATE repeatable ritual value

The Dragon can be resummoned with End Crystals.

Subsequent kills:

- provide repeat encounters;
- create additional End gateways up to the vanilla limit;
- allow more Dragon's Breath collection.

This is a real repeatable loop, but unlikely to sustain broad logistics by itself.

### Endermen / pearls / XP — STRONG farm value, weak geographic value

The End is highly suitable for Enderman farms.

This gives:

- Ender Pearls;
- XP.

This is a strong reason to maintain at least one End facility.

But a single optimized farm can satisfy the need, so it does not automatically justify a rich province network.

### Chorus — MODERATE renewable material value

Chorus can provide:

- food/teleport behavior;
- Popped Chorus Fruit;
- Purpur;
- End Rod-related building use.

It is renewable and can support End agriculture.

However it can be relocated after acquisition.

Therefore it supports End identity and settlement but is not a strong geographic monopoly.

## Advanced propulsion caution

Create Propulsion: Simulated is a promising late-flight candidate because it includes:

- chemical thrusters;
- solid-fuel thrusters;
- ion thrusters;
- optional pressure-dependent performance.

Its source tests support a possible low-pressure specialization.

However:

> The current Ion Thruster recipe is not End-gated.

Current recipe uses engineering components such as:

- Redstone Accumulator;
- Brass Sheet;
- Redstone Inductor;
- Sturdy Sheet;
- Precision Mechanism;
- Chute.

The Vector Thruster then adds Platinum components.

Therefore Skyforge must **not** currently claim:

~~~text
reach End
-> unlock ion propulsion
~~~

unless:

- another dependency supplies that progression;
- a minimal recipe integration is intentionally added;
- or actual gameplay makes the connection through some other existing requirement.

Levitite is the genuine current End-specific Aeronautics reward.

## Sable pressure envelope

All Sable built-in vanilla dimensions lose pressure upward.

The End is not unique in this respect.

Current End profile is approximately:

~~~text
Y 0     1.0000
Y 200   0.4493
Y 216   0.4215
Y 256   0.0000
~~~

Compared with the Overworld:

~~~text
pressure ~= 1.0 around Y 63
~~~

the End may place ordinary island operations in a lower-pressure regime at comparable positive Y.

Because Sable scales:

- wing lift;
- aerodynamic drag;
- propeller thrust;

by local pressure, altitude can matter.

### What must actually be proven

The design should only elevate End pressure into a major gameplay identity if:

~~~text
same representative craft
    Overworld ordinary operating altitude
vs
    End ordinary operating altitude

produces a noticeable engineering/operational difference
~~~

If not, do not manufacture difficulty merely to justify a narrative.

## Repeatable reasons to remain

### Strong current loops

- Shulker farming;
- Enderman farming;
- Dragon respawning;
- Dragon's Breath;
- Chorus farming;
- ongoing End City/Ship expeditions;
- End Stone extraction for Levitite if demand is high enough.

### Weak current loops

- ordinary local mining diversity;
- broad industrial feedstocks;
- multi-node resource trade;
- faction/civilization interaction;
- routine fluid/bulk export;
- locally unique processing that cannot simply move home.

This means the End's long-term value naturally leans toward:

~~~text
EXPEDITION
+ SPECIALIZED ENGINEERING
+ FORWARD INFRASTRUCTURE
~~~

rather than:

~~~text
bulk commodity economy
~~~

That is acceptable.

The End does not need to imitate the Overworld economy.

## Infrastructure worth building

A fully realized End should plausibly reward:

### Central End terminal

After Dragon victory:

- portal/gateway staging;
- storage;
- aircraft assembly/repair;
- rescue capability;
- maps/navigation records.

### Outer-End forward base

- landing/staging;
- repair;
- food;
- water imported/stored;
- spare vehicle;
- recovery equipment.

### Navigation beacons

The End's sparse landmarks and void should make:

- route markers;
- beacon chains;
- coordinates;
- radar/CC systems;

particularly valuable.

### End Stone quarry / Levitite plant

If Levitite demand is vehicle-scale:

~~~text
quarry
-> crusher
-> imported zinc/water
-> heated mixing
-> crystallization
-> aircraft production
~~~

This could become the End's strongest industrial base loop.

### Shulker facility

A permanent Shulker farm is a legitimate End-specific infrastructure site.

### Expedition airfield / depot

A forward network can support:

- longer range;
- city hunting;
- salvage;
- rare site exploration;
- rescue.

The End can justify multiple bases through **distance and recovery**, even without many bulk resources.

## Engineering specialization

The End should create **expeditionary aircraft design**.

Possible constraints:

- long gaps;
- sparse emergency landing;
- pressure envelope;
- limited resupply;
- severe void loss;
- uncertain destination geometry.

This encourages:

- range;
- redundancy;
- navigation;
- fuel margin;
- rescue planning;
- Levitite-supported weight management;
- robust low-speed handling;
- multiple propulsion/control strategies.

### Vehicle classes that may emerge

~~~text
SCOUT
    fast
    light
    high range
    low payload

EXPEDITION AIRCRAFT
    navigation
    redundancy
    supplies
    recovery margin

LEVITITE UTILITY CRAFT
    passive weight support
    hover/stationkeeping potential
    separate climb propulsion

HEAVY END CARRIER
    cargo / mobile workshop
    likely infrastructure-dependent

RESCUE CRAFT
    redundancy
    passenger retrieval
    high reliability
~~~

Do not hardcode these classes.

Let physics + route structure make them rational.

## Personal mobility versus aircraft

Elytra should remain useful.

Healthy division:

~~~text
ELYTRA
    personal scout
    emergency recovery
    local/long personal crossing
    low infrastructure

AIRCRAFT
    passengers
    freight
    workshop capability
    safer repeat operation
    instruments
    route persistence

LEVITITE-ASSISTED AIRCRAFT
    new weight-support architecture
~~~

This keeps End loot relevant without collapsing the Aeronautics layer.

## Structures and site value

### Central Dragon arena

This remains a dedicated ritual special site.

Do not generalize it as an ordinary random landmass.

### End gateways

Gateways are progression and topology.

A Skyforge outer End should treat destination safety/placement as a first-class requirement.

### End Cities

Full Skyforge authorship can improve them by making their locations:

- silhouette-significant;
- expedition-worthy;
- terrain-compatible;
- part of route planning.

Do not overpopulate them.

Rarity is part of their value.

### End Ships

Ships are particularly compatible with the late aviation fantasy.

They can remain:

- rare visual targets;
- Elytra sources;
- evidence of strange aerial infrastructure.

### Future exceptional sites

The End is the dimension most able to support extremely sparse, strange, high-value exceptional content.

But do not fill emptiness simply because Skyforge can.

Negative space is gameplay.

## Ecology

The End should remain biologically sparse.

Existing strong identities:

- Endermen;
- Chorus;
- Shulkers at cities.

Potential future additions should be:

- specialist;
- exceptional;
- alien;
- low-density.

Do not import ordinary Overworld food-web complexity.

A full End realization earns value through:

- geometry;
- engineering;
- exploration;
- ritual;
- exceptional biology;

not biodiversity count.

## Threat identity

The primary End threat should remain:

- void;
- range;
- recovery;
- navigation;
- Endermen;
- Shulker sites;
- Dragon/exceptional encounters.

This is qualitatively different from Nether ambient hostility.

The End should often feel **quietly dangerous**.

A long uneventful crossing can still be tense if failure is consequential.

## Failure and recovery

This is one of the End's most important gameplay systems.

A vehicle failure over the void can be catastrophic.

Therefore full End gameplay needs recovery tools.

Potential layers:

- Elytra backup;
- Ender Pearls;
- spare propulsion;
- multiple power sources;
- forward depots;
- rescue aircraft;
- Shulker-carried emergency kit;
- waypoint network;
- intermediate landing nodes.

The objective is not safety.

It is **risk the player can engineer against**.

That is central to the End's value.

## Cross-dimensional economic role

The End should export:

- Levitite / End-derived lift-support material;
- Shulker Shells/Boxes;
- Elytra;
- End City salvage;
- Dragon's Breath;
- Chorus/Purpur/End materials;
- exceptional End loot.

It should import:

- zinc;
- water;
- food;
- Create machinery;
- fuel/power;
- repair parts;
- navigation infrastructure.

This creates a strong expeditionary supply chain.

### Important asymmetry

Unlike the Nether, the End does not need to export huge tonnage to justify itself.

A dimension can be economically important through:

- high-value specialist material;
- unique vehicle capability;
- rare loot;
- infrastructure-intensive exploration.

The key is that the player makes **many decisions and trips**, not necessarily many bulk-freight stacks.

## The "worth" problem

The End fails full-realization value if the optimal player loop remains:

~~~text
kill Dragon
-> get Elytra
-> get enough Shulker Boxes
-> make a little Levitite
-> never build there
~~~

The End passes if normal mature play tends toward:

~~~text
central terminal
+ forward bases
+ navigation network
+ repeated city/exceptional expeditions
+ Shulker/Enderman infrastructure
+ significant Levitite production
+ End-specialized aircraft
~~~

Not every player must do all of this.

Enough of it must be rational and rewarding that the dimension supports deep play.

## Current value sufficiency

### Unique progression — VERY STRONG

- Dragon;
- gateways;
- Elytra;
- Shulkers;
- End Cities.

### Unique engineering capability — VERY STRONG

- Levitite is a genuine new vehicle architecture input.

### Repeatable economy — MODERATE

Strong farms and repeated expeditions exist.

Broad industrial demand is not yet proven.

### Infrastructure demand — MODERATE/STRONG POTENTIAL

Distance, navigation, recovery, Levitite processing, and farms can support it.

### Engineering specialization — VERY STRONG POTENTIAL

Void + pressure envelope + Levitite + range/recovery is a distinct design space.

### Civilization/structure depth — MODERATE

End Cities are strong exceptional sites but there is no broad living civilization network.

This is appropriate if intentional.

### Ecological depth — LOW BY DESIGN

This is not a weakness if the End remains alien and sparse.

## Main gameplay risks

### Risk E-1 — one-and-done loot dimension

The player gets Elytra/Shulkers and leaves.

**Mitigation:** Levitite demand, forward expedition network, farms, exceptional sites.

### Risk E-2 — Levitite is too cheap

A small amount of End Stone creates all the lift material the player ever needs.

**Mitigation:** measure real craft-scale Levitite demand before changing recipes.

### Risk E-3 — Levitite replaces every lift system

All aircraft become floating slabs.

**Mitigation:** preserve no-free-climb and low-speed handling cost; test wings/airships/hybrids.

### Risk E-4 — End aircraft are just Overworld aircraft with bigger fuel tanks

Dimension has no engineering identity.

**Mitigation:** test actual pressure envelope, staging/recovery, Levitite, route geometry.

### Risk E-5 — void punishment kills experimentation

One failure deletes too much progress.

**Mitigation:** recoverability infrastructure and backup systems.

### Risk E-6 — too many landmarks destroy the End

Filling empty space makes the dimension feel like another province network.

**Mitigation:** preserve large negative space; make destinations rare and meaningful.

### Risk E-7 — advanced propulsion is falsely End-gated

Current Propulsion Ion Thruster recipe does not require End materials.

**Mitigation:** do not claim that progression without a deliberate integration decision.

## Third-party enrichment direction

Current 1.21.1 NeoForge audit identifies:

- **Unusual End** as the leading broad End behavior/content prototype because it combines configurable generation changes, Create compatibility, distinctive mobs, counterplay, maps/information, and varied structures;
- **MES / Moog's End Structures** as a narrow structure supplement if needed;
- **Enderman Overhaul** as a creature/reward A/B whose special pearls require mobility-governance review;
- **Bosses of Mass Destruction** as a sparse exceptional boss/site layer;
- **End's Delight** as an optional local-life/sustenance layer if expedition bases feel too sterile.

Do not use multiple broad End overhauls merely to increase biome count.

The End should gain **rare contrast**, not uniform richness.

## Full-realization minimum

Before committing to full outer-End authorship, prove at least:

1. **Dragon/gateway progression** remains intact;
2. **Levitite** produces a meaningful new aircraft architecture;
3. **one repeatable End farm** remains worthwhile;
4. **repeated expedition value** exists beyond the first End City;
5. **forward infrastructure** measurably improves operations;
6. **navigation/recovery** creates interesting engineering decisions;
7. **Elytra and aircraft coexist** without one erasing the other;
8. **outer-End terrain** creates real route decisions;
9. **Levitite demand** is large enough to make End material processing relevant, or another enduring End-specific capability fills that role.

The first eight look promising now.

The ninth is the major economic unknown.

## Skyforge worldgen payoff

Skyforge can add unusual value to the End by explicitly composing:

- negative space;
- destination spacing;
- visible landmarks;
- vertical route options;
- gateway placement;
- safe/unsafe staging;
- End City admissibility;
- exceptional sites;
- recovery topology.

This is precisely the kind of problem where random island noise is least expressive.

The End may therefore be the **lowest-risk second Skyforge dimension** geometrically while still producing a strongly different game.

## Priority recommendation

Proceed with the outer End as the **first cross-dimension Skyforge pilot**, but make the pilot answer gameplay questions rather than merely prove geometry.

Required pilot evidence:

~~~text
same mature Overworld aircraft
    -> End route

Levitite-supported variant
    -> same / harder route

gateway arrival
    -> safe staging

End City target
    -> navigation / landing / recovery

failure case
    -> plausible rescue
~~~

Only after this should full outer-End terrain vocabulary be locked.

## Strategic conclusion

The End has enough **latent** value to justify serious realization work.

Its strongest identity is:

> **a frontier where distance, failure, and strange lift technology force mature aviation to evolve.**

What remains to prove is whether the End supplies enough **ongoing** value for that evolved aviation to keep operating there.

## Acceptance principle

> The End earns full realization when reaching one distant city is an achievement, but building the system that makes the next ten expeditions possible is the real progression.
