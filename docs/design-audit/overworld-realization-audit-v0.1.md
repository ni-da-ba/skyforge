# Overworld Realization Audit v0.1

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-05
**Status:** Detailed gameplay/value audit. The Overworld clearly justifies full Skyforge realization.

## Audit question

> Does the Overworld provide enough unique, repeatable, infrastructure-generating gameplay to justify full Skyforge authorship?

**Verdict: YES — STRONG / FOUNDATIONAL.**

The Overworld is not merely one of three dimensions.

It is the primary world in which the player:

- survives;
- settles;
- learns Skyforge geography;
- learns atmospheric movement;
- develops Create engineering;
- reaches first powered flight;
- builds resource routes;
- operates civilization-scale logistics.

The design risk is not lack of value.

The design risk is **concentrating so much value here that Nether and End become optional errands**.

## Gameplay identity

Working role:

> **Build a life, learn the sky, and turn geography into a network.**

The Overworld should be the broadest and most internally complete dimension.

It supports the widest range of playstyles:

- survival;
- construction;
- farming;
- exploration;
- mining;
- ecology;
- trade;
- Create engineering;
- aviation;
- logistics;
- civilization interaction;
- weather/navigation;
- long-term settlement.

This breadth is desirable because it provides the player's home context.

It should not imply that every advanced capability is obtainable without leaving it.

## Entry and progression role

The player begins here.

Therefore the Overworld carries the strongest anti-soft-lock obligations.

### P0 — survival completeness

A normal starting region must support:

- food;
- water;
- wood;
- stone;
- shelter;
- iron-class tools;
- renewable ordinary fuel.

### P0.5 — cheap personal crossing

Potential early movement:

- walking;
- bridging;
- climbing;
- local rope/lift solutions;
- cheap glider;
- thermals/updrafts.

### P1 — Create bootstrap

The starting cluster should permit:

- basic Create machinery;
- iron + andesite / Andesite Alloy;
- wool/fiber aerodynamic material;
- adhesive path;
- ordinary fuel;
- first workshop.

### P2 — first powered flight

The current leading first powered aircraft is intentionally:

- pre-brass;
- pre-petroleum;
- pre-electricity;
- pre-Nether;
- pre-End.

This is fundamental.

The player should solve the sky **before** the game asks them to solve dimensional progression.

### P2+ — regional specialization

After flight:

- copper;
- zinc;
- Brass;
- redstone;
- richer mining regions;
- petroleum;
- electrical/instrumentation materials;

can become distributed economic geography.

## Unique capability payoffs

The Overworld does not need one single headline unlock.

Its value comes from being the **platform on which most capabilities are assembled into systems**.

### First practical aviation

The Overworld is where the player learns:

- aerodynamic lift;
- thrust;
- control surfaces;
- launch/landing;
- aircraft recovery;
- cargo;
- weather;
- route selection.

This is a uniquely important progression event.

### Atmosphere literacy

Current desired progression:

~~~text
vegetation / particles
    -> surface wind

smoke
    -> wind + local rising air

thermal-soaring birds
    -> usable lift

clouds / weather
    -> larger atmospheric organization

instruments
    -> quantitative interpretation
~~~

The Overworld is the primary place where this literacy is learned.

### Regional industrial geography

The Overworld should host the largest range of:

- mines;
- farms;
- oilfields;
- refineries;
- workshops;
- airfields;
- regional hubs;
- trade routes.

This makes the player practice logistics before entering dimensions whose logistics are more specialized.

## Repeatable reasons to remain

The Overworld easily passes the repeatability test.

### Agriculture and biological production

Renewable production includes:

- food;
- wood;
- fiber/wool;
- livestock;
- biomass;
- ecological products;
- selected modded crops/animals.

These support permanent settlement.

### Mining and raw material extraction

Regional mining can sustain:

- iron;
- copper;
- zinc;
- redstone;
- gold;
- diamond;
- construction stone;
- selected industrial minerals.

### Strategic petroleum

If Create Diesel Generators remains selected, petroleum is a strong strategic-node loop:

~~~text
oilfield
    -> extraction
    -> refinery
    -> tank storage
    -> fuel depot
    -> aircraft / industrial distribution
~~~

This is one of the clearest reasons for mature freight infrastructure.

### Trade and civilization

Generated civilization can support repeat interaction through:

- markets;
- repair;
- fuel;
- maps/navigation;
- information;
- replenishment;
- regional specialization.

The civilization system should demonstrate logistics rather than replace player agency.

### Weather and route operation

A route is not solved permanently merely because two airports exist.

Weather/wind can preserve operational decisions:

- depart now or wait;
- climb or stay low;
- detour;
- choose a safer aircraft;
- use instrumented navigation.

This makes mature infrastructure operational rather than purely decorative.

## Engineering specialization

The Overworld should favor **general-purpose open-sky aircraft**.

Relevant assumptions:

- large open air volume;
- terrestrial weather;
- normal Sable altitude-pressure curve;
- thermals/ridge lift;
- long sightlines;
- a wide range of landing environments.

Healthy aircraft diversity:

~~~text
small personal aircraft
cargo aircraft
airships
specialized survey craft
tankers
utility VTOL
large mobile platforms
~~~

No single form should dominate.

- glider/Elytra;
- Shulkers later.

### Vehicle freight

- bulk blocks;
- fluid tanks;
- entities;

### Network freight

- repeat routes;
- automation;
- multiple origins/destinations;

Aircraft should win increasingly by **throughput and payload class**, not simply by raw item slots.

## Structures and encounter value

The Overworld has abundant vanilla and selected-mod structure potential.

Important vanilla progression/exceptional content includes:

- villages;
- strongholds;
- trial chambers;
- ancient cities;
- mineshafts;
- dungeons;
- monuments/mansions and other region-specific structures.

For Skyforge the rule remains:

> Structures are consequences of site meaning and geometry, not independent random decoration.

### Stronghold

The stronghold is especially important because it provides the transition to the End.

A full Skyforge Overworld must preserve:

- reliable progression access;
- interpretable placement;
- valid portal room geometry.

### Trial chambers / exceptional underground content

Current Java 1.21 gameplay gives trial chambers unique progression rewards such as Breeze Rods and the Heavy Core/Mace path.

These are useful evidence that the Overworld's underground cannot be treated as filler beneath floating islands.

Skyforge cave/subsurface authorship must support meaningful deep destinations.

## Ecology

The Overworld has by far the richest ecological role.

Current leading realization stack:

- Naturalist;
- Fowl Play;
- Critters & Companions where useful;
- Sky Whales;
- bespoke cliff raptor only if required.

Ecology should derive from:

- climate;
- water;
- vegetation;
- surface area;
- cliffs;
- caves;
- altitude;
- isolation;
- human disturbance.

The Overworld should feel biologically broad while each individual island remains sparse.

### Atmospheric ecology

The adapted red-tailed-hawk / thermal-soarer concept is especially valuable because it connects:

~~~text
ecology
    -> atmosphere
    -> player observation
    -> glider gameplay
~~~

That kind of cross-system meaning is a strong argument for full Skyforge authorship.

## Threat identity

The Overworld should not be universally hostile.

Threat is spatially and semantically variable.

Potential sources:

- darkness;
- wilderness;
- hostile structures;
- faction-controlled regions;
- dangerous fauna;
- weather;
- cliff/void exposure;
- legendary sites.

The player should be able to build safe civilization.

This contrasts with the Nether's baseline environmental hostility.

## Failure and recovery

Because the Overworld is the home dimension, it must provide the strongest recovery ecosystem.

Expected resilience:

- spare low-tier aircraft;
- gliders;
- salvageable wrecks where possible;
- repair materials;
- settlements;
- distributed storage;
- rescue routes.

A crash should be costly without routinely producing an unrecoverable campaign collapse.

## Cross-dimensional role

The Overworld should export:

- general industrial capacity;
- zinc/copper/brass;
- food;
- ordinary construction material;
- aircraft components;
- petroleum/refined fuels where relevant;
- tools and logistics infrastructure.

It should import:

- Blaze/Nether processing capability;
- quartz;
- Netherite;
- Wolframite/Tungsten if selected;
- End Stone-derived Levitite;

This creates a healthy pattern:

~~~text
Overworld
    = industrial home base

Nether / End
    = specialized inputs and engineering domains
~~~

But some processing and infrastructure should remain worth building inside the other dimensions too.

## Main gameplay risks

### Risk O-1 — Overworld monopolizes value

If every advanced material and capability is obtainable here, Nether/End become optional.

**Mitigation:** reserve genuinely useful dimensional capabilities without making them first-flight prerequisites.

### Risk O-2 — aviation erases geography

If first aircraft makes every destination equally cheap, regional composition loses meaning.

**Mitigation:** cargo, fuel, weather, landing, navigation, and throughput remain important.

### Risk O-3 — civilization overpopulation

Too many settlements turn wilderness into suburbs.

**Mitigation:** sparse network; functionally significant nodes.

### Risk O-4 — resource clutter

Guaranteeing every resource everywhere destroys trade pressure.

**Mitigation:** local bootstrap completeness, regional specialization later.

### Risk O-5 — island sameness

Even excellent generation fails if all islands look like variants of one blob.

**Mitigation:** authored hierarchy and morphology diversity.

The Overworld already exceeds the threshold for full Skyforge authorship.

## Minimum acceptance before showcase

Before calling the Overworld experience realized enough for showcase:

1. starter-region bootstrap works;
2. glider/thermal loop reads correctly;
3. first powered aircraft works;
4. regional resource differentiation is visible;
5. at least one settlement/industrial network is legible;
6. one meaningful cargo route exists;
7. ecology does not overpopulate;
8. horizon/negative-space composition is strong;
9. caves/subsurface content remains meaningful;
10. Distant Horizons presentation is convincing.

## Strategic conclusion

The Overworld is the **reference implementation and permanent home economy**.

Its job is not to be the hardest dimension.

Its job is to make the player care enough about the world that later dimensions feel like extensions of a civilization they have actually built.

## Acceptance principle

> The Overworld earns full realization by being the place where survival becomes infrastructure and infrastructure becomes a world.
