# CIV-0005: Open Sky emblem lock

**Status:** Accepted design precommit
**Date:** 2026-09-10

## Decision

The Skyfarer's Guild uses one canonical institutional emblem: **Open Sky**.

Core invariant geometry:
- open circular sky field;
- central navigation star / beacon;
- paired upward-opening wing forms;
- open lower horizon / cradle gesture.

The wings are stylized heraldic forms rather than a literal bird. The symbol must avoid reading primarily as national, royal, or military heraldry.

Core color identity:
- deep navy;
- aged brass / muted gold;
- warm local neutral materials.

Regional and contextual variants may alter material realization, secondary chapter devices, border patterns, supporting colors, wear, and mounting architecture, but may not replace the canonical Open Sky geometry.

The emblem should exist in ceremonial, standard, utility, signal/navigation, monochrome/carved, and low-resolution Minecraft realizations, all visibly traceable to the same mark.

Exact color values, Minecraft block choices, and final production geometry remain implementation-stage validation items, not open conceptual questions.
