# CIV-0004: Guild recognition and regional contexts

**Status:** Precommitted visual-language guidance
**Stage:** Pre-civilization; implementation deferred
**Date:** 2026-09-10

## Purpose

This record defines how one canonical Skyfarer's Guild identity remains recognizable across materially and culturally distinct regions.

## Recognition principle

A regional Guild building should answer two questions simultaneously:

- **Who operates this place?** — answered by the invariant Open Sky emblem and standardized Guild service grammar.
- **Where is this place?** — answered by local materials, climate adaptations, construction traditions, secondary chapter devices, weathering, and district context.

Regional variation may never replace the canonical Open Sky emblem.

## Context matrix

| Context | Typical Guild functions | Local expression | Guild expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northern / alpine | navigation, rescue, freight relay, weather service | heavy stone, steep roofs, snow sheds, wind bracing | dark iron + brass crest, compact beacon, mountain secondary device |
| Coastal / maritime-derived | freight, fishery trade, dispatch, storm rescue | weathered timber, copper, dock forms, mast language | navy/brass signs, wave/current secondary device, mast flags |
| Frontier | registry, contracts, basic repair, mail, emergency aid | rough local timber/stone, repaired additions, simple sheds | painted or stamped Open Sky mark, one banner, standardized service plates |
| Dryland / desert | provisioning, long-haul freight, navigation | pale masonry, shaded arcades, recessed openings, dust screens | bronze/brass plaque, reinforced cloth standard, horizon/mesa secondary device |
| Industrial | heavy repair, certification, freight, manufacturing | brick/stone/steel, gantries, yards, vents | enamel/riveted crest plates, inspection markings, machinery-scale utility emblem |
| Agricultural | procurement, storage, contracts, provisioning | timber, granaries, silos, market yards | formal depot signage, grain/leaf secondary device, civic-scale crest |
| Scholastic / navigational | academy, charting, survey, meteorology | observatory, library, collegiate and instrument motifs | polished crest, chart/astrolabe secondary device, restrained ceremonial treatment |
| Salvage / recovery | rescue, claims, wreck recovery, Sky Dredger support | yards, cranes, workshops, emergency infrastructure | high-visibility operational markings around the unchanged Open Sky emblem |

## Common operational contexts

### Guild hall / registry
Most formal local expression. The Open Sky emblem should be plainly visible from the public approach. Service counters, contract boards, registry plates, and chapter identifiers reinforce institutional continuity.

### Freight warehouse
Architecture may be overwhelmingly local or industrial, but loading interfaces, cargo markings, lamps, inspection plates, and crest signage are standardized.

### Skyport / terminal
The strongest long-distance recognition context. Signal mast, banners, route boards, docking hardware, and large-scale emblem placement should make Guild operation legible before the player reaches street level.

### Aircraft / vessel
Use the standard or utility Open Sky mark. Chapter marks may appear separately as small identifiers; they should not be fused into a radically different logo.

### Rescue / salvage equipment
Operational visibility may introduce safety orange, white, or other high-contrast local markings, but these remain accents surrounding the navy/brass identity or monochrome Open Sky geometry.

### Documents / seals / inspection plates
Use simplified or monochrome geometry. The emblem must survive stamping, carving, etching, and very low-resolution reproduction.

## Design constraint

The Guild should feel like an institution that has occupied and upgraded local buildings for generations. New standardized fittings may sit on old local shells. This historical accretion is desirable and should be preserved rather than visually cleaned into one universal architectural style.
